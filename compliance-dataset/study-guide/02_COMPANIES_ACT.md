# Part 2 — Companies Act, 2013: The Backbone of Corporate Compliance

## 2.1 Overview

The Companies Act, 2013 (CA 2013) is the primary legislation governing all companies in India. For a forensic analyst, this is the single most important law — nearly every company document you examine must comply with it.

**Your pipeline category**: `companies_act_2013`

## 2.2 Critical Sections for Document Compliance

### Financial Statements & Books of Account (S.128-134)

| Section | What It Requires | What to Check |
|---|---|---|
| **S.128** | Maintain books of account at registered office, accrual basis, double-entry | Are books maintained properly? Accrual basis used? |
| **S.129** | Financial statements must give true and fair view, comply with accounting standards | Does the P&L/BS follow Schedule III format? Ind AS compliance? |
| **S.130** | Re-opening accounts only by court/tribunal order | Any unauthorized restatement of prior years? |
| **S.131** | Voluntary revision requires Tribunal approval | Any revised financials without tribunal order? |
| **S.133** | Central Govt prescribes accounting standards (via ICAI/NFRA) | Which accounting standards apply? Are they followed? |
| **S.134** | Board's Report requirements, Director's Responsibility Statement | Does Board report contain all mandatory disclosures? |
| **S.135** | CSR — 2% of average net profits if thresholds met | CSR policy disclosed? 2% spend verified? |

### Audit Framework (S.139-147)

| Section | What It Requires | What to Check |
|---|---|---|
| **S.139** | Appointment of auditors (5-year term, rotation for listed cos) | Auditor rotation compliance? |
| **S.143** | Auditor's powers, duties, CARO reporting, fraud reporting | Does audit report cover CARO? S.143(12) fraud reporting done? |
| **S.144** | Prohibited services by auditors | Is auditor providing prohibited services? |
| **S.147** | Penalties for auditor contravention | — |

### Corporate Governance (S.149-197)

| Section | What It Requires | What to Check |
|---|---|---|
| **S.149** | Board composition, independent directors | Minimum IDs met? |
| **S.177** | Audit Committee — composition, terms of reference | Audit Committee constituted? Functioning per ToR? |
| **S.178** | Nomination & Remuneration Committee | Committee exists with required composition? |
| **S.188** | Related Party Transactions — Board/shareholder approval | RPTs disclosed? Prior approval obtained? |
| **S.204** | Secretarial Audit (mandatory for listed + prescribed companies) | Secretarial audit report filed? |

### Fraud & Investigation (S.211-229)

| Section | What It Requires | What to Check |
|---|---|---|
| **S.211** | SFIO establishment | — |

## 2.3 Schedule III — Financial Statement Format

Schedule III of CA 2013 prescribes the **exact format** for Balance Sheet and P&L. This is what your pipeline should check financial statements against. Key requirements:
- Balance Sheet must follow vertical format with prescribed heads
- P&L must show revenue from operations separately from other income
- Notes to accounts must follow prescribed order
- Comparative figures for previous year mandatory
