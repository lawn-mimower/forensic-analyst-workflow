## Deep Dive Track (1 month+)

For comprehensive expertise:

### Phase 1 (Weeks 1-2): Complete Foundation Track above

### Phase 2 (Week 3): Audit Standards Deep Dive
- Download and study ICAI Checklist on 38 SAs (from ICAI website)
- Focus on SA 240 (Fraud), SA 250 (Laws), SA 315 (Risk Assessment), SA 550 (Related Parties)
- Read `ICAI_Audit_Committee_Technical_Guide.pdf`
- Study ICSI CSAS-4 standard

### Phase 3 (Week 4): Specialized Areas
- Study NFRA AQR reports (from nfra.gov.in) — real-world audit failures
- Study RBI Master Directions for banking compliance
- Study SEBI Master Circulars for listed company compliance
- Explore Indian Kanoon API for case law on key sections
- Study `ICSI_SACMDD_Study_Material.pdf` for due diligence methodology

### Ongoing: Stay Current
- Subscribe to ICAI, ICSI, SEBI, and RBI updates
- Monitor NFRA enforcement orders (precedent-setting)
- Track Finance Act changes annually (affects tax audit requirements)
- Watch for FAIS updates (currently ~20 of planned 30 standards issued)
