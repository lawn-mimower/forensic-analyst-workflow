## Corporate information

Northfield Fabricators Private Limited ("the Company") is a private limited company incorporated in 2015 with its registered office at Hosur, Tamil Nadu. Its shares are not listed on any stock exchange. The Company fabricates sheet-metal enclosures and brackets for electrical equipment makers. The directors of the Company are Prakash Iyer (Managing Director), Lakshmi Iyer (Director).

## Directors' report (extract) for the year ended 31 March 2025

### Financial performance

Revenue from operations for the year was Rs. 846.10 lakhs against Rs. 812.40 lakhs in the previous year, and the profit for the year was Rs. 177.05 lakhs against Rs. 30.87 lakhs. Profit improved on account of better material yields during the year. The Board does not recommend a dividend.
