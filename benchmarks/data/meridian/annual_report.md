## Corporate information

Meridian Agro Exports Private Limited ("the Company") is a private limited company incorporated in 2011 with its registered office at Kochi, Kerala. Its shares are not listed on any stock exchange. The Company processes and exports whole and ground spices. The directors of the Company are Thomas Varghese (Managing Director), Maria Varghese (Director), Ravi Menon (Director).
