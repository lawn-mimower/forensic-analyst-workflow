## Corporate information

Vardhan Precision Castings Private Limited ("the Company") is a private limited company incorporated in 2004 with its registered office at Rajkot, Gujarat. Its shares are not listed on any stock exchange. The Company produces investment castings for pumps, valves and auto components. The directors of the Company are Harsh Vardhan Shah (Managing Director), Nisha Shah (Director), Imran Patel (Director).

## Directors' report (extract) for the year ended 31 March 2025

### Financial performance

Revenue from operations for the year was Rs. 5,124.60 lakhs against Rs. 4,702.35 lakhs in the previous year, and the profit for the year was Rs. 360.31 lakhs against Rs. 307.06 lakhs. The Board does not recommend a dividend.

### Particulars of contracts or arrangements with related parties (Section 188)

The purchase of a vacuum casting line from Vardhan Engineering Works for Rs. 352.80 lakhs exceeded the threshold in Rule 15 of the Companies (Meetings of Board and its Powers) Rules, 2014. It was approved by the Board on 30 July 2024 and by the members by special resolution at the extraordinary general meeting held on 20 August 2024, and the particulars are given in Form AOC-2 annexed to this report. All other related party transactions were in the ordinary course of business and on an arm's length basis.

### Internal financial controls

The Company has documented policies for purchases and payments. Any single payment above Rs. 100,000 requires the approval of two directors. The Board considers the internal financial controls to be adequate and operating effectively.

### Deposits

The Company has not accepted any deposits from the public within the meaning of Chapter V of the Companies Act, 2013. During the year the Company did not accept any loan or deposit in cash; all borrowings were received through banking channels.
