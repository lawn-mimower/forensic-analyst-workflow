## Corporate information

Kestrel Polymers Private Limited ("the Company") is a private limited company incorporated in 2009 with its registered office at Nashik, Maharashtra. Its shares are not listed on any stock exchange. The Company manufactures injection-moulded plastic components for appliance makers. The directors of the Company are Rohan Kulkarni (Managing Director), Sneha Kulkarni (Director), Vivek Joshi (Director).
