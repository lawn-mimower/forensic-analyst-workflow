## Corporate information

Tarangini Foods Private Limited ("the Company") is a private limited company incorporated in 2012 with its registered office at Mysuru, Karnataka. Its shares are not listed on any stock exchange. The Company makes and sells packaged snacks and ready-to-eat savouries. The directors of the Company are Suresh Hegde (Managing Director), Anjali Hegde (Director), Farhan Qureshi (Director).

## Directors' report (extract) for the year ended 31 March 2025

### Financial performance

Revenue from operations for the year was Rs. 2,964.80 lakhs against Rs. 2,718.35 lakhs in the previous year, and the profit for the year was Rs. 217.90 lakhs against Rs. 184.49 lakhs. The Board does not recommend a dividend.
